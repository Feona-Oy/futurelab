A few useful details

Questions, answered

A little more about getting started.

Does my teenager need previous experience?

No previous experience is needed to get started. The professional introduces the task and guides participants through the process.

What if the field is not the right fit?

That is a useful discovery too. The aim is to explore, not to commit. Reflecting on what did not appeal can help shape the next experience.

What are the fees and program duration?

Fees and duration depend on the program. Request the current details so you can review the format, schedule, and cost before making a decision.

Who guides the sessions?

Industry professionals introduce their fields, explain practical tasks, and share feedback on participants’ work.

Can we ask for help choosing a program?

Yes. Select “I would like help choosing” when making an enquiry. You do not need to arrive with a fixed career goal.
